Bloodstream Command is a real-time strategy game set inside a living body. You command the defensive cells: drag to select them, then tap an infection focus and they stream along the veins to destroy it. The beating heart shifts the tempo, toxic veins and clots slow your cells, and armored, healing and mutant foci fight back. Keep the body's integrity above zero and clear every focus across 16 hand-tuned levels. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
