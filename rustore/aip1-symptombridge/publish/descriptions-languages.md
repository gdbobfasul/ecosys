```
Symptom Bridge helps you turn what you feel into a clear, well-ordered summary to hand your doctor, so nothing important gets forgotten in a short appointment. Answer a few guided questions or type freely, and the app organises your complaint into structured sections a clinician expects — main complaint, onset and course, location, intensity, character, what makes it better or worse, associated symptoms, current medications and allergies.
```
