Fair Split is a fair-division assistant that settles who owes whom and who gets what. Split shared expenses and instantly see the smallest set of transfers that balances the group; divide inheritance or shared items fairly so everyone feels they got their fair share; and share out chores evenly. An optional, privacy-first AI helper can suggest fair value weightings and explain the result in plain words. It works fully offline: you can save and reload cases on the device, and no account or sign-in is ever required.
