Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1skillcoach.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Skill Coach turns any skill into a hands-on, learn-by-doing plan. Tell it what you want to get better at — drawing, guitar, public speaking, cooking, coding or anything else — and it builds you a personalized 6-day micro-program of short daily practice tasks, each with a clear done-criterion so you always know when you have finished. It follows the science of deliberate practice: small, focused reps, honest self-rating after each task (too hard, just right, too easy) and steady progress you can see. It works fully offline with built-in expert programs for popular skills; optionally add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) and it will generate a tailored program for absolutely any skill you name. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline using built-in practice programs. Optionally you may add YOUR OWN AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you ask the app to generate or coach a program, and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the skill name and your practice notes you chose to send are transmitted to the AI provider CHOSEN BY YOU to return the program; nothing else is transmitted. Without a key the app runs entirely offline. There are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking, and progress is stored only on the device.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
