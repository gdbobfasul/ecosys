Rewind The Cause is a side-scrolling game where time runs backwards. You walk back through a collapsing street, un-fire your shot so the bullet returns to the barrel, and every fallen figure you reach stands up alive again. Each revival stitches the rift in time shut. Race back to the very first second across 15 hand-tuned levels with rewinding rubble, rolling wreckage, reversing cars and backward blasts. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
