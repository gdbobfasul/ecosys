Seasongate is a side-scrolling quest where you switch the four seasons to reshape the world. A warrior marches across a wild land to a distant tower to free a captured princess. Every gate is blocked by something one season opens and another seals: freeze a river to ice in winter, douse a wildfire with the spring downpour, bake the mud solid in summer, wither the thorns in autumn. Fight off beasts — wolf packs, snakes, hornet swarms and crows — where only the right season drives them away, dodge avalanches, floods and spreading fire, free prisoners from cages and watchtowers, and win a weapon reward on the way. Switch the season at the right moment across 18 hand-tuned levels — the number of switches is limited. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
