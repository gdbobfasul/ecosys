Echo Duel is a hunt in total darkness where the enemy gives itself away only by sound: a pulse of waves that quickly fades. Click the centre of the sound to strike — the more precise the click, the harder the hit — then dodge left or right to slip the counter-attack, and manage your distance on the vertical axis. Fight through 100 levels across 10 upgrade decades, each granting you a new tool while the enemy gains a new power. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
