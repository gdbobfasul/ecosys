Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1plainaction.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Plain Action turns any confusing document into a plain-language summary and a clear checklist of what you actually need to do. Paste a message, email, contract, official letter, bill, terms of service or notice and it explains it in simple words, then lists concrete action items with any deadlines, flags what to watch out for, and can draft a reply or response for you. It works fully offline with built-in logic; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) for deeper, AI-assisted summaries and drafts. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline using built-in logic. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press the analyze/simplify action and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the document text you chose to process is sent to the AI provider you selected to return the summary and checklist; nothing else is transmitted, and there are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
