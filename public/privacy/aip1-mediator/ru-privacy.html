Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1mediator.rustore · Store: RuStore · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Fair Mediator helps two people settle a disagreement calmly and fairly. Describe the argument from both sides — what happened, what each person feels and wants — and it acts as a neutral, non-violent-communication mediator: a balanced summary that blames no one, the real needs behind each position, the common ground you already share, and concrete, respectful next steps and phrases you can actually say. Great for couples, family, friends, flatmates, co-workers and neighbours who want to de-escalate instead of win. It works fully offline with built-in mediation guidance; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) for a deeper, AI-assisted mediation. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline using built-in mediation guidance. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press the mediate/analyze button and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the conflict text you chose to submit is sent to the AI provider you selected to return the mediation; nothing else is transmitted, and there are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
