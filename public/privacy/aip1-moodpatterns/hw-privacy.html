Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1moodpatterns.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Mood Patterns is a private mood and emotion journal that helps you understand how you really feel over time. Log your mood in seconds, add tags for what happened (sleep, work, people, weather, health), and see your mood charted day by day together with the patterns and tag correlations that shape it — which situations lift you up and which drag you down. Everything is stored locally on your device and nothing is ever sent anywhere without your own key and your explicit approval. Optionally add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept only on your device) to turn your entries into gentle AI-assisted weekly and monthly insights; without a key the app still works fully offline with its built-in summaries. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages. Mood Patterns is a self-reflection and journaling tool for general wellbeing — it is not medical or psychological advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline and keeps your mood journal only on your device. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press an AI-insight action, and only if you have configured such a key or endpoint, the entries you chose to analyze are sent to the AI provider you selected to return the insight; nothing else is transmitted, and there are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking. Mood Patterns is a self-reflection tool and is not medical or psychological advice.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
