Features

🛡 Theft protection (Vault Guard)

  • A large transfer is held, not executed immediately
  • The guardian can cancel a suspicious held transfer
  • Holder threshold: 0 HRVS · treasury: 20,000 HRVS
  • Waiting period: 60 min

🏦 Fund on transfer

  • 2% of every transfer goes to a transparent fund
  • Powers rewards / buybacks

⚙ Managed by the bot

  • The bot watches held transfers in real time
  • It auto-cancels suspicious outgoing transfers from the treasury
  • It records statistics and suggests legitimate moves
  • The protection is against thieves — not against the owner
