## Български (bg) — AppGallery: **Bulgarian**
**Brief introduction (до 25 знака):**
```
Private AI companion that you
```
**Full introduction:**
```
Pupikes Learning Buddy is a private AI companion that you name and teach yourself: it talks with you by voice or text, learns knowledge from the web, and can see through the camera. Everything it learns stays on your device, tied to a secret code word only you know.

There is no developer account or sign-in; access is guarded by a code word you choose. The companion’s name, everything it learns, your conversations and habits are stored only on your device and are not uploaded by us.

Everything runs on your device with no account and no ads; the interface supports 15 languages.

Support: miroljubkalaydjiev177@gmail.com
```
