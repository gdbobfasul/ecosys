# selflearning-friend — Reviewer note (Huawei AppGallery)
Version 1.0036

## For reviewer
Reply to rule 4.3 ("similar to existing apps; improve interaction design and feature depth").
Pupikes LB is not a chat front-end to a cloud model. Version 1.0036 rebuilds its core around one idea that no other app on AppGallery offers: a companion that knows your life and advises you from it — everything on the device, no account, nothing uploaded. The first screen after start is the new ADVISOR; the older functions remain as secondary tabs.
- Advisor (first tab) — ask what to buy, learn, sell or do (typed or dictated, or from the chat: "should I…"). It weighs "for" and "against" from what it holds about you: your memory facts and preferences, your Companion entries (people involved, lessons, your own rating), your Notary deals (only if unlocked and permitted), what it has learned from sources/packs/YouTube and your own lessons, plus your previous decisions. Every point states where it comes from; with no data it honestly says "I do not know enough". Decisions are kept in a history where you rate "how it went" (well / so-so / badly) with a note — similar future questions use that feedback, and per-type statistics ("of your 4 purchase decisions, 3 went badly") appear as arguments. An "About you" view shows exactly which local sources the advice draws on.
- Companion — the important moments of your life, not the last hour: Life story (timeline of birth, school, wedding, children, moves, accidents), Work experience (where you worked, what you learned and how, problems you solved) and Life experience (who you dealt with, who lied to or cheated you, wrong steps, close relationships). Entries carry date, people, lesson and your positive/negative/neutral rating; search by word, person or year; a "people in your life" summary shows who appears in good or bad experiences.
- Notary — registry and confessor: Will (who gets what and why, who is excluded and why), Deals (purchases, sales, bigger deals with amount, counterparty and gain/loss outcome) and Confessions (where you went wrong, what you wanted to do). It has its own PIN, separate from the code word; the content is encrypted on the device with WebCrypto (PBKDF2 → AES-GCM 256) and only ciphertext is stored; it auto-locks when the app goes to the background; export is an encrypted file that can be imported back with the same PIN; the Advisor may read it only while unlocked and only if you switch that on. No backup key exists — the app says so plainly.
All three work fully offline from local data only; nothing is fetched from the network. No new Android permissions are required. The app keeps its existing features: voice/text chat, self-learning from free sources, knowledge packs, camera vision, tasks, YouTube learning, animations, the Study tab (quiz, progress, diary, teach me, glossary), backup/restore and a 15-language interface.
