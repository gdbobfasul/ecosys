New in version 1.0021 — "Guardian": the app now also watches over you. If the phone is untouched for N hours (you set them, with sleep hours), it first asks you "Are you OK?" with a loud signal and a big "Yes" button; with no answer within M minutes it sends your chosen loved ones a message with the time of last activity, battery level, location (optional) and who wrote urgently in the meantime — through our chat, through the loved one's latest messenger notification, or as a ready SMS to send. Check log, "guardian active" indicator, "simulate silence" test. Delegate: urgent messages (keywords or VIP) are forwarded to a chosen person and the sender receives "<name> will answer you". Reply in the sender's language: the language of the incoming message is detected on the device and the robot replies with the template in that language (15 languages); your own text is translated once and remembered.

An automatic message-reply assistant that answers for you by your own rules: keyword triggers with ready replies, office hours, away messages, and allow/block lists. It logs every auto-reply and notifies you, and includes a built-in demo inbox so you can test everything instantly.

New in version 1.0020: quiet hours (the robot stays silent at night) and a vacation mode with an end date; a library of ready replies by category (work, vacation, driving, meeting, urgent) with variables for name, time, date and vacation end; contact groups / VIP with a separate reply or no auto-reply; a "once per N hours per sender" limit and a reply delay; statistics by day (chart), channel, rule and sender with CSV export of the log; a "simulate incoming" test that shows which rule and which reply would fire. Everything runs on the device, no network.
