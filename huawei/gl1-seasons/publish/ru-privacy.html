Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.gl1seasons.rustore · Store: RuStore · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Seasongate is a side-scrolling quest where you switch the four seasons to reshape the world. A warrior marches across a wild land to a distant tower to free a captured princess. Every gate is blocked by something one season opens and another seals: freeze a river to ice in winter, douse a wildfire with the spring downpour, bake the mud solid in summer, wither the thorns in autumn. Fight off beasts — wolf packs, snakes, hornet swarms and crows — where only the right season drives them away, dodge avalanches, floods and spreading fire, free prisoners from cages and watchtowers, and win a weapon reward on the way. Switch the season at the right moment across 18 hand-tuned levels — the number of switches is limited. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
