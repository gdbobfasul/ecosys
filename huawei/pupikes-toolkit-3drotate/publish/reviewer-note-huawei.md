## For reviewer

This app's MAIN feature is Pupikes Paper 3D — it turns a photo into a printable PAPER 3D figure (cube, pyramid, gift box, lantern, photo stand, pop-up card) that you fold and hold in your hand, with a live 3D preview of the assembled figure and a printable stereo card. This is NOT a generic image/rotate tool; the classic rotate/crop/resize tools are secondary. The home screen leads directly into Paper 3D: pick a photo, choose a figure, get an A4/Letter print template with fold lines, numbered glue tabs and assembly instructions in the app language — everything on-device, no account, no network.

The secondary offline "Tools" section (Canvas API) — image transformer (rotate/flip with live preview), aspect-preserving resizer, and an eyedropper color picker (HEX/RGB) — remains fully offline, works without an account or network, and is localized. Paper 3D is the distinctive core that differentiates the app.
