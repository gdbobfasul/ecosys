Fair Mediator helps two people settle a disagreement calmly and fairly. Describe the argument from both sides — what happened, what each person feels and wants — and it acts as a neutral, non-violent-communication mediator: a balanced summary that blames no one, the real needs behind each position, the common ground you already share, and concrete, respectful next steps and phrases you can actually say. Great for couples, family, friends, flatmates, co-workers and neighbours who want to de-escalate instead of win. It works fully offline with built-in mediation guidance; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) for a deeper, AI-assisted mediation. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.
