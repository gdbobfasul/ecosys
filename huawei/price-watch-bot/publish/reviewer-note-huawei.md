## For reviewer

A new "Tools" (Инструменти) section adds three real, fully-offline shopping utilities independent of price-watching: (1) a unit-price comparator (лв/кг, лв/л, лв/бр) that highlights the cheapest option, (2) a discount calculator working both directions (price↔%), (3) a shopping list with a budget, running total, and over-budget warning. Local storage, copy/export. No network. This provides distinctive multi-feature content.
