# Reviewer note — Pupikes Price Watch (Huawei)

## For reviewer
This version significantly enriches the app to address rule 4.1 ("single feature"). The app now has a top tab navigation with 7 sections, not one:

1. Watch — the original price/threshold alerts (crypto + fiat).
2. Futures — per-coin funding rate, open interest and long/short ratio (market-pressure / liquidation risk overview).
3. Greed — the crypto Fear & Greed index with a gauge.
4. RSI — Relative Strength Index (14) for BTC/ETH/ZEC across 4h / 24h / 1 week / 1 month, flagging oversold/overbought.
5. Stocks — live quotes for major indices and stocks (S&P 500, QQQ, Dow, Russell 2000, NVDA, AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN, TSM, META, TSLA, AMD, MSFT).
6. Indices — global crypto indicators (total market cap, 24h volume, BTC/ETH dominance, active coins, Fear & Greed).
7. Market — top-50 coin list with price, 1h/24h/7d change, market cap and 24h volume.

All data comes from free public APIs (Binance, CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance, alternative.me) — no account, no keys, read-only, no trading/wallet. 15-language interface, no ads. The store icon is also re-uploaded identical for all listing languages (rule 1.20).
