```
Pupikes Price Watch is an all-in-one market monitor with 13 sections. Set price alerts on cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE) and fiat exchange rates and get a local notification the moment a threshold you choose is crossed. Then explore the market: a live top-50 coin list with price and 1h/24h/7d change; top gainers and losers; trending coins; crypto futures (funding rate, open interest, long/short ratio); the Fear & Greed index; RSI for BTC/ETH/ZEC across 4h, 24h, 1 week and 1 month with oversold/overbought flags; global crypto indices (total market cap, BTC and ETH dominance); major stocks and indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, NVDA, AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN, META, TSLA and more); world markets (Nikkei, DAX, FTSE, Hang Seng, CAC); commodities (gold, silver, oil, copper); macro (VIX volatility, US Treasury yields, US Dollar Index); and forex pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and more).
```
