New in version 1.0021 — a new core: COST IN LIFE. Fill in your profile (income, working hours, vacation, sleep, family support, living expenses, loans, cash, movable and real property, goals — or take the figures from Personal budget and Planner) and any price is converted into your own life: working hours and days, full salaries, days of life, nights of sleep while you earn it, share of your remaining working life and of your net worth, pay-off time from free money, extra hours if bought on credit, delay of each goal and the shrink of your no-work runway. Type the price or photograph a price tag / ad and pick the recognized amount. “My time”: estimated remaining years, waking and working hours, how long your money lasts without work, and a money-versus-age chart for “work until…”, “stop in N years” and “+ this purchase”. Analyses are kept in a history. Everything stays on the device.
