# pupikes-toolkit-finance — Reviewer note (Huawei AppGallery)
Version 1.0021

## For reviewer
Reply to rule 4.3 ("similar to existing apps; improve interaction design and feature depth").
Version 1.0021 gives the app a NEW CORE that no budget or finance tracker offers: COST IN LIFE — an instant analyzer that translates the price of any product into the user's OWN LIFE TIME, computed from a personal profile (income, working hours, vacation days, sleep, family dependents and their support, living expenses, loan payments and balance, cash, movable and real property, saving goals; the figures can be imported with one tap from the Personal budget and Planner tools). It is the first screen after start — the home screen embeds it and the older tools are cards below it. Sub-screens:
- Analyzer: type a price or photograph a price tag / classified ad and pick the recognized amount → working hours and working days, full salaries, days of life, nights of sleep while earning it, share of the remaining working life and of net worth, pay-off time from free money (and with goals paused), monthly payment and extra hours of interest if bought on credit, the delay of every saving goal and how the "no-work runway" shrinks; a light / medium / heavy verdict; save to history.
- Profile: the inputs above plus goals (amount + monthly saving with an ETA), one-tap import from Personal budget (envelopes, recurring items, assets and liabilities) and from Planner (debts); a live summary "your hour" (earning per working hour, fixed outgoings, free money per month).
- My time: estimated remaining years (world average by age, editable), waking and working hours left, sleep / work / free split of the remaining life, how long the money lasts without work (cash only, or selling all property), and a canvas chart of money against age for three scenarios — "work until retirement age", "stop working in N years", "+ this purchase" — with the age at which money runs out or the amount left at the end of life.
- History: saved analyses (item, price, working hours, pay-off time).
Everything is computed and stored on the device; no account, nothing uploaded. No new permissions: the photo comes from the standard system picker (no CAMERA permission), and the text-recognition module is fetched once from a CDN only when the user taps the photo button; without connection the price is entered manually.
The app keeps its personal budget, financial planner, live currency/crypto converter, crypto charts, 20-pair watchlist and calculators, with a 15-language interface — described in the store listing.
