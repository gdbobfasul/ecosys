## Български (bg) — AppGallery: **Bulgarian**
**Brief introduction (до 25 знака):**
```
Pupikes Toolkit Finance
```
**Full introduction:**
```
Pupikes Toolkit Finance bundles the money tools: live currency rates and a converter, crypto charts with indicators, a 20-currency watchlist with alerts, and loan/VAT/interest calculators.

Watch lists, thresholds and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account. The app only reads public market data.

Everything runs on your device with no account and no ads; the interface supports 15 languages.

Support: miroljubkalaydjiev177@gmail.com
```
