## For reviewer

To address the feature-depth note, a new offline "Tools" (Инструменти) section was added: a text analyzer (chars/words/lines/paragraphs, page & reading-time estimates), text cleaner/formatter (case, whitespace, line ops), and a document outline planner with reorder and export. All tools are fully offline, work without an account or network, and are localized. This adds distinctive, deeper functionality that differentiates the app.
