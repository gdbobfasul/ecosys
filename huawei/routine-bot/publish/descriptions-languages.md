**Brief introduction (до 25 знака):**
```
Personal daily assistant
```
**Full introduction:**
```
Pupikes Routine Planner is a personal daily assistant: a morning briefing (weather, agenda, motivation), reminders for medication, habits and tasks, an optional evening summary, and it can read your notes aloud in 15 languages.

Your reminders, notes, habits and settings are stored only on your device. Text-to-speech uses your device’s speaker. There is no account and no cloud.

Everything runs on your device with no account and no ads; the interface supports 15 languages.
