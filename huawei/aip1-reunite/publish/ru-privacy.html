Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1reunite.rustore · Store: RuStore · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Reunite is a lost-and-found network that helps missing pets, people, documents and belongings find their way home. Post what you lost or what you found — with a short description, category, place and date — and Reunite instantly compares it, on your device, against the other posts and surfaces the most likely matches with a similarity score, so a lost brown dog near the park meets the found brown dog by the lake. Browse and filter lost vs found by category, get an alert when a strong new match appears, and reach out to reunite. It works fully offline with built-in concept matching; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) for smarter, AI-assisted match explanations. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline using built-in on-device concept matching, and your posts stay only on your device. Optionally you may add YOUR OWN AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you explicitly request an AI-assisted match explanation and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the lost/found descriptions you chose are sent to the AI provider you selected to return the explanation; nothing else is transmitted. Without an AI key the app runs entirely offline. There are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking, and we do not run our own server.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
