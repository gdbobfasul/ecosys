Shadowbound is a runner with a twist: you lead only the shadow of a man walking a clear road above. Every obstacle lives in the shadow's plane — jump the rocks and pits, duck under the beams. When the shadow stumbles, the real man above staggers, bleeds and can die. Guide the shadow through 18 hand-tuned levels with pits, double sections, moving obstacles and flickering light. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
