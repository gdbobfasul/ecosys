Symptom Bridge helps you turn what you feel into a clear, well-ordered summary to hand your doctor, so nothing important gets forgotten in a short appointment. Answer a few guided questions or type freely, and the app organises your complaint into structured sections a clinician expects — main complaint, onset and course, location, intensity, character, what makes it better or worse, associated symptoms, current medications and allergies. Keep separate profiles for family members, save each visit to a local history, and copy or print the summary to show or read out to the doctor. It works fully offline; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) to have free-text tidied and phrased more clearly. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT diagnose or give medical advice — it only helps you communicate with your doctor. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.
