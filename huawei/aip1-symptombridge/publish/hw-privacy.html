Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1symptombridge.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Symptom Bridge helps you turn what you feel into a clear, well-ordered summary to hand your doctor, so nothing important gets forgotten in a short appointment. Answer a few guided questions or type freely, and the app organises your complaint into structured sections a clinician expects — main complaint, onset and course, location, intensity, character, what makes it better or worse, associated symptoms, current medications and allergies. Keep separate profiles for family members, save each visit to a local history, and copy or print the summary to show or read out to the doctor. It works fully offline; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) to have free-text tidied and phrased more clearly. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT diagnose or give medical advice — it only helps you communicate with your doctor. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. Your health information (complaints, profiles and visit history) is stored only locally on your device and is never sent to us; we have no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press the AI action, and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the symptom text you chose is sent to the AI provider you selected in order to tidy the wording; without a key the app works offline. Symptom Bridge is NOT a medical device and does NOT diagnose or provide medical advice — it only helps organise your communication with your doctor.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
