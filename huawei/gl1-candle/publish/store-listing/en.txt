Eternal Flame is a reflex game about keeping a single candle burning in the dark. Threats close in from every side, each announced on the rim before it strikes: wind, whirlwinds and thieving torches from the left and right, water, rain and downpours from above, swords, stones and arrows head-on. Meet each one with the right guard for the right direction — raise the giant hand to the side, cover the flame with your palm from above, or lift the shield up front — and hold the guard through storms. Survive across 12 hand-tuned nights with wider windows, longer holds and a short all-blocking dome you earn along the way. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
