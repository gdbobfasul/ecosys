## For reviewer

To address the review, a new offline "Tools" (Инструменти) section was added with distinctive reading utilities: an article analyzer (word/sentence counts, reading time, readability estimate), a summary/outline builder (pick key sentences), and a personal glossary. All on-device, no network. All tools are fully offline, work without an account or network, and are localized. This adds distinctive, deeper functionality that differentiates the app.
