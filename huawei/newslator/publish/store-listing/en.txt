World news from ~198 countries, in your language

Read the news of any country in the world — translated into your language, or read aloud to you.

What you can do:
• Browse news from ~198 countries worldwide
• Get multiple public sources per country (official RSS + Google News)
• Translate any headline or article into 15 languages with one tap
• Listen to articles read aloud (text-to-speech)
• Save articles and read them later in your offline library
• Share articles to other apps
• Search the news and browse by category
• Use the app in 15 interface languages

• Tools: compare two countries side by side (headlines in their own language)
• Country pulse: exchange rates and capital weather
• Daily digest fetched once at start — readable offline and aloud
• Keyword alerts: matches from the digest with a notification
• New data is fetched once per launch — saves traffic
• Home dashboard on the first screen: cards for every feature with live content — news, compare, pulse, digest, alerts, saved, search
• Built-in offline edition: ~2000 headlines from 20 countries ship inside the app — works without internet, marked “offline edition from <date>”

NewsLator gathers public news from official and a few unofficial sources. The sources belong to their owners; this app is an independent reader and is not affiliated with them.
