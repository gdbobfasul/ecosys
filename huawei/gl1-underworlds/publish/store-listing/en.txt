Core Descent is a mining puzzle: dig your way straight down to the glowing core of the Earth. Every level is a hand-built shaft of soil, rock, ore, crystal, magma and caves. Pick the right tool for each obstacle — pickaxe and shovel for stone, a drill for solid walls, a heat suit for lava, a detonator for gunpowder, a pump for flooded tunnels, a rope for chasms and a lamp for the dark — and clear a safe path to the bottom. Every level is solvable. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
