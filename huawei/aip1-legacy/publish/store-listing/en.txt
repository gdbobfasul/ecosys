Legacy Keeper is a calm, private place to put your affairs in order — your last wishes, the people who matter, where your important assets and documents are, and the messages you want your loved ones to read one day. Instead of scattered notes, you keep everything in one clear, organized keepsake on your own device: heirs and who gets what, accounts and property, insurance and passwords hints, funeral preferences, and heartfelt letters. It works fully offline; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) and the app will help you draft, phrase and structure each section in your own words. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages. This is a personal organizer for your intentions — it is not a legally valid will; for legal force, consult a notary or lawyer.
