Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1legacy.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Legacy Keeper is a calm, private place to put your affairs in order — your last wishes, the people who matter, where your important assets and documents are, and the messages you want your loved ones to read one day. Instead of scattered notes, you keep everything in one clear, organized keepsake on your own device: heirs and who gets what, accounts and property, insurance and passwords hints, funeral preferences, and heartfelt letters. It works fully offline; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) and the app will help you draft, phrase and structure each section in your own words. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages. This is a personal organizer for your intentions — it is not a legally valid will; for legal force, consult a notary or lawyer.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. Everything you type — wishes, names, assets, documents, letters — is stored only locally on your device and is never sent anywhere by default; the app works fully offline. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press an AI Draft/Help action and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the text you chose for that section is sent to the AI provider you selected to return the drafted text; nothing else is transmitted, and there are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking. The app is a personal organizer of intentions and is not a legally valid will.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
