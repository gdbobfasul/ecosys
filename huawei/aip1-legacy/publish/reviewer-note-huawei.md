## For reviewer

To address the review, a new "Tools" (Инструменти) section has been added in addition to the main screen, so the app is far more than a notes app: (1) an asset/estate inventory with per-item values and an auto-sum total, (2) a beneficiaries list with contacts and messages, (3) an "Important documents" readiness checklist with a progress bar and custom items. All fully offline (local storage), with copy/export. Distinctive, interactive, deeper functionality.
