# Бележка за модератора (Huawei) — services-toolkit (Pupikes Toolkit)
Version 1.0021

## For reviewer
Reply to rule 4.3 ("similar to existing apps; improve interaction design and feature depth").

Pupikes Toolkit is the UNION of the whole Pupikes Toolkit family. Two members of the family — Pupikes Toolkit Text and Pupikes Toolkit Passwords — are already approved on AppGallery; this app contains them in full, together with the other family apps (PDF, QR, Pictures, Videos, Finance, Scraper, 3D Rotate, AI Announcement, Sound). Version 1.0021 brings every tool of the family into one app: 57 tools in 10 sections, all built in-house, all on the device, no account, no third-party SDKs, 15-language interface. The home screen is a grid grouped by sections; on top of it sits "Tool chains", a feature none of the family apps has on its own.

All tools by section:
1. Documents (5): Sealed Docs — a closed circle for documents (on first use the phone creates its own ECDH/ECDSA P-256 key pair, groups are gathered by scanning the members' QR codes, every file is sealed with AES-256-GCM and signed; nothing goes through a server); PDF studio (pages with thumbnails, photos/text → PDF, stamp, N per sheet, info); PDF tools (merge, split, watermark); PDF compress; PDF → Word.
2. Text (14, = Pupikes Toolkit Text): counter and formatting; case & lines (18 operations, in-place with Undo); encode/decode (Base64/URL/HTML/hex); generator (lorem/lists); extract e-mails/links/numbers; diff; Unicode; JSON format/validate; word frequency; Roman numerals; number bases; wrap; text colour; timestamps.
3. Passwords & keys (9, = Pupikes Toolkit Passwords + authenticator): password analyzer (entropy, crack time offline/online, weaknesses, HIBP k-anonymity check); generator with 9 methods; passphrases; AES-256 encrypt/decrypt of notes; hashes; import and audit of browser passwords (Chrome/Firefox/Edge CSV — duplicates, weak, old); bulk generator; recovery FAQ; authenticator (TOTP two-factor codes).
4. Photos & video (4): How-to video — shoot or pick a few clips/photos as STEPS and the app builds a lesson with chapters (Step 1, 2, 3…), a caption per step in any of the 15 languages, arrows/circles/underlines on the frame, title and recap screens, optional voice-over (microphone or built-in speech synthesis) and generated background music; one project exports a video lesson (MP4), a GIF guide and a printable sheet (PDF/PNG); a clearly marked sample guide is loaded on first open; Photo evidence — cases (rental, car, delivery, repair, meter readings, insurance damage) where every photo gets a SHA-256 fingerprint of its original bytes, a watermark with date/time/place and fingerprint, and an entry in a tamper-evident hash-chained journal; "Verify" finds a photo by fingerprint or shows a difference map for an edited copy; signed PDF report with finger signatures; one sample case on first open; photo workbench (compress with quality/format/max width, rotate/mirror, centred crop 1:1/4:3/3:4/16:9/9:16, exact size, filters, batch, EXIF removed on save); video workbench (trim, audio → MP3, rotate/mirror, speed 0.5×–3×, remove sound, 720p/480p, compress, frame → JPG (now taken by the device decoder, it no longer hangs), GIF, format conversion; ffmpeg engine bundled).
5. Sound (1): Sound & Voice Studio — 10 one-tap voice modulators (robot, chipmunk, deep voice, telephone, radio, hall, cave, choir, alien, clean noise), 21 stackable effects with sliders (pitch without tempo change, tempo, echo, reverb, EQ, spectral noise reduction, fade, reverse, normalize…), microphone recording, before/after waveforms, save as WAV/MP3/OGG/M4A; the format converter remains as a secondary tab. A synthesized example is loaded on open so the Studio works with no file and no network.
6. Finance (8): Cost in life (the price of anything translated into hours/days of the user's own life from a personal profile, with a "My time" chart and history); personal budget with envelopes; planner for debts and goals; calculators (loan, VAT, interest, percentages); currency rates (fiat + crypto); crypto charts (RSI, Fibonacci, BTC/ETH multi-year history); 20-pair watchlist with alerts; Price Watch (per-country price tracking with notifications).
7. QR codes (10): QR labels for home and inventory (item cards with photo/place/expiry/lending, printed A4/Letter label sheets, scan → card, 6 sample items on first launch); QR generator and reader; My codes library; batch; styled; Wi-Fi; contact; event; payment; place.
8. Web (3): page watcher (saved targets, scan history, NEW/GONE/CHANGED diff of prices/e-mails/phones/headings, notifications, CSV export, 2 built-in sample pages that work offline); on-device scraper with "Try with an example"; scraper through a service (user-configured backend URL).
9. 3D (1): Paper 3D — from a photo to a paper figure (cube, pyramid, gift box, lantern, stand, pop-up card): print template with cut/fold lines and glue tabs, WebGL 3D preview, stereo card; the 3D rotation workbench (volume/relief, exact angles, perspective straightening, 360° GIF) stays as secondary tabs.
10. AI & business texts (2): announcement campaigns (type × tone × up to 15 languages → text, saved as a campaign with channel, date/time, repeat and reminder, history and statistics; direct AI → relay → BUILT-IN offline generator, so it never fails with a connection error; 3 sample campaigns on first launch); AI text helper (write / summarize / translate / explain, same fallback).

Tool chains (the highlighted card at the top of the home screen): one tool's output is the next tool's input. Every tool saves its result as a file on the device (or copies it) and the next tool opens it from the file picker; a bar at the top of each tool shows "Chain — step i of N" with a hint and a "Next step" button. Six ready chains by situation (paper → protected PDF; offer for a client; new account — safely; home inventory; big purchase; video for social media) and the user's own chains (name + any sequence of the 57 tools), stored on the device.

New in this build: "How-to video" (from Pupikes Toolkit Videos) and "Photo evidence" (from Pupikes Toolkit Pictures) are now in the "Photos & video" section, so the union again contains every function of the family.

Fixed in this version: "Crypto charts" no longer throws on the BTC/ETH history panels (a local variable shadowed the translation function).

Permissions: CAMERA — only to scan QR codes (Sealed Docs group ceremony, QR reader, QR labels); RECORD_AUDIO + MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — only for the microphone recording in the Sound & Voice Studio and the optional voice-over in "How-to video"; ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — only when the user turns on "Record location (GPS)" in the Settings tab of "Photo evidence" (off by default), used only to write coordinates into the watermark, the journal and the PDF report on the device, never sent anywhere; POST_NOTIFICATIONS — price/page-watch/campaign reminders. No permission is requested before the user taps the corresponding button. Network is used only by the tools marked "online" (rates, crypto charts, price watch, page watcher for remote pages, scraper, AI); everything else works with Wi-Fi off.

## How to test
Choose any language on the first screen → the home screen shows the grid grouped by 10 sections (Documents, Text, Passwords & keys, Photos & video, Sound, Finance, QR codes, Web, 3D, AI & business texts) with the highlighted card "Tool chains" on top. The search box filters all 57 tools.
1. Tool chains: tap "Tool chains" → "Ready chains" → "New account — safely" → "Start chain" → the Password analyzer opens with a bar "Chain 'New account — safely' — step 1 of 4" and a hint. Type "Summer2019!" → verdict, entropy and crack time appear. Tap "Next step →" → the Password generator opens (step 2 of 4) → "Generate" → tap "Next step →" → Encrypt (step 3) → "Next step →" → Authenticator (step 4) → "Finish chain ✓" → "Chain finished". "My chains" → "New chain" → name + "+ Add step" twice → "Save chain" → the chain appears with "Start chain".
2. Documents → "Sealed Docs": the screen opens with sample groups and a feed (marked as samples); "My QR" tab shows the phone's public key as a QR. "PDF studio" → "Photos" tab → pick photos → "Build PDF" → the PDF is saved/shared.
3. Text → "Case & lines": type a text → "UPPER" → the text changes in place; "Undo" restores it. Any other text tool works the same way, fully offline.
4. Passwords & keys → "Password analyzer": type "Summer2019!" → WEAK, entropy, crack time, weaknesses; "Check in leaked databases" → result. "Browser passwords" → pick a Chrome CSV export → duplicates/weak/old are listed.
5. Photos & video → "Photo workbench": pick a photo → filter "Sepia" → "Process" → before/after with sizes → save. "Video workbench": pick a video → operation "Trim" → "Run" → progress bar → result saved/shared.
6. Sound → "Sound & Voice Studio": the example is already loaded; tap "🤖 Robot" → the "After" waveform changes → "▶ Result". "🎙 Record" → "Start recording" → allow the microphone → "Stop" → "To the Studio".
7. Finance → "Cost in life": type an item and a price → "Analyze" → working hours, days of life, verdict. "Personal budget" and "Planner" open with sample envelopes/goals (marked as samples).
8. QR codes → "QR labels": 6 sample items; open one → item card with QR; "Print" tab → sheet preview → "Download PNG/PDF". "QR code" → type a text → QR appears; "Scan" uses the camera.
9. Web → "Page watcher": 2 sample targets → "Scan all" → the history shows what changed (works offline on the built-in pages). "On-device scraper" → "Try with an example".
10. 3D → "Paper 3D": choose a figure → put a photo on a side → "Preview" (rotate with a finger) → "Print sheet" → PNG/PDF template.
11. AI & business texts → "Announcement campaigns": "Example" → "Generate" → the text appears even with Wi-Fi off (built-in generator) → "Save campaign" → "List" / "History" / "Statistics" tabs.
12. Photos & video → "How-to video": a SAMPLE guide ("Quick 5-minute omelette", 4 illustrated steps) is already loaded. Tap "▶ Play the lesson" → title screen, Steps 1–4 with captions and animated marks, recap screen. Tap "✏ Mark on the frame" on a step → draw an arrow → it appears on the frame. Under "Export the lesson" tap "🎬 Video lesson (MP4)" → progress, then the video is shown and saved/shared; "📄 Printable sheet (PDF)" → A4 sheet with numbered steps. "🎙 Record voice" asks for the microphone only at that moment.
13. Photos & video → "Photo evidence": one SAMPLE case with 5 photos; open it → each photo has a short SHA-256 and journal number; tap a photo → watermark preview → "Save with watermark". Tab "Verify" → "Try with an example" → difference map with the changed area and the verdict "LOCAL CHANGE". Tab "Journal" → "Journal intact". Case → "PDF report" → draw a signature → "Create signed PDF". Tab "Settings" → "Record location (GPS)" asks for location only when turned on.
14. Photos & video → "Video workbench": choose a video → operation "Frame → photo (JPG)" → "Run" → a JPG is saved in about a second.
