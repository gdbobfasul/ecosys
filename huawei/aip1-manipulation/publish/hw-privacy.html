Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1manipulation.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Manipulation Shield helps you spot when a message, ad, chat, email or contract is trying to manipulate you. Paste any text and it flags manipulation tactics — false urgency, artificial scarcity, fear and guilt tripping, flattery, fake authority, social pressure, too-good-to-be-true offers, scam and phishing patterns, hidden contract clauses and logical fallacies — with a clear risk level and per-tactic explanations, so you can decide with a cool head. It works fully offline with built-in heuristics; optionally you can add your own AI key or proxy endpoint (kept locally on your device) for a deeper AI-assisted analysis. No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works fully offline using built-in heuristics. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key or proxy endpoint, which is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you press Analyze and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the text you chose to analyze is sent to the AI provider you selected to return the analysis; nothing else is transmitted, and there are no accounts, ads or third-party tracking.

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
