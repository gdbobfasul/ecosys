# camera-watch — Reviewer note (Huawei AppGallery)
Version 1.0021

## For reviewer
Reply to rule 4.3 ("similar to existing apps; improve interaction design and feature depth").
Version 1.0021 gives the app a new core that no camera or motion-detector app offers: MotionSecurityHawk — a mentor and guardian for a person who goes out alone (a child from age 7, an elderly relative walking to the shop) and for the relative who watches over them. The first screen asks which role this phone has: "Wearer" (goes with the person) or "Guardian" (stays with the parent); two phones are paired by a short code and the wearer must tick an on-screen consent (a "tracking is on" indicator stays visible, and a "Stop tracking" button ends it at any time). Everything below is implemented and working in this build:
1. Movement map — the wearer's GPS way is drawn continuously on a canvas (scale bar, north arrow, start/last point, accuracy circle) and appears on the guardian's phone with the last place and "open in maps" links, so the person cannot get lost and, if something happens, the trail and last position are with the guardian.
2. Voice guard — the microphone listens continuously; on sound it records short segments (up to 15 s); a scream/sharp noise, or help/threat/"where are they taking me" keywords in 15 languages (matched on the device via speech recognition where the WebView provides it; on devices without speech recognition the guard works by sound), raise an alert to the guardian with the recording, the words and the place.
3. Task ("recipe") — the guardian sets a destination, an instruction ("buy bread, pay, come home") and home; the wearer's phone guides by arrow and distance, detects arrival by GPS, repeats the instruction aloud three times (text-to-speech in the app language), then guides back and confirms the return. The guardian can also send a spoken message and a "where are you?" request.
4. HELP button on the wearer's phone; automatic alert on a fall/impact from the accelerometer; allowed zone (geofence) set by the guardian with a spoken "go back" and an alert on leaving it.
The previous camera guard (motion with on-device recognition, timeline, zones, schedule, time-lapse, statistics, alarm) remains as the "Camera" section.
Privacy and permissions: ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION and RECORD_AUDIO are new in this version and are requested only in the "Wearer" role after the on-screen consent; nothing is used in the "Guardian" or "Camera" roles. All packets are encrypted on the device (AES-GCM, key derived from the pairing code); our relay only forwards opaque encrypted packets between the two paired phones, keeps them in memory for at most 24 hours and cannot read them. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases; 15-language interface. Tracking works while the wearer's screen is open (the app keeps the screen awake); there is no background service.
