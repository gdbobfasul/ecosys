About the app: Pupikes Baby Radar is a local baby/room audio-video monitor.
Two devices on the same network/account pair (one as the baby unit, one as
the parent viewer); audio and motion alerts are sent to the parent device.
No account registration beyond pairing, no ads, 15-language interface.
