## For reviewer

To address the feature-depth note, a new offline "Tools" (Инструменти) section was added: a baby diary (feeding/sleep/diaper log with daily summary), an offline white/pink-noise soother with a sleep-timer (Web Audio, no audio files), and a feeding/growth guidance calculator (clearly marked as guidance, not medical advice). All tools are fully offline, work without an account or network, and are localized. This adds distinctive, deeper functionality that differentiates the app.
