## For reviewer

A new "Tools" (Инструменти) section adds three real, fully-offline utilities independent of the AI feature: (1) a library of 7 ready announcement templates (editable, saved locally), (2) offline text formatting with live character/word counts and per-platform limits (X 280, SMS 160, Instagram, SEO), plus case tools, (3) a reusable hashtag/emoji helper. No network. This enriches the app well beyond a single simple feature.
