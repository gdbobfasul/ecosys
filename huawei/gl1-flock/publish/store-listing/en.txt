Flock Drift is a gentle steering game: guide your migrating flock of birds through drifting wind, dunes and night skies while keeping the formation together. Lead the V-formation, ride tailwinds, dodge predators and rocks and gather stragglers before they fall behind, across a series of hand-tuned migration levels. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
