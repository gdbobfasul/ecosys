Pupikes Auth & Passwords is an encrypted password manager, a two-factor authenticator (TOTP/HOTP/Steam) and a key converter between apps — all in one vault on your device, with no account, no server and no ads.

Passwords: site, login, password, other code, notes; import and export from Chrome, Edge and Firefox (CSV); duplicate finder; global search across every section. 2FA: add by camera, QR image, manually or an otpauth:// link; convert both ways with Google Authenticator (migration QR), Aegis (plain or encrypted), 2FAS (plain or encrypted), otpauth lists, JSON and QR images in a .zip. Crypto: seed phrases, private keys, PINs and addresses organised by 32 built-in wallets (MetaMask, Ledger, Trezor, Binance, Trust Wallet…) plus up to 20 custom ones; separate sections for SSH access, EVM networks and ERC-20 tokens; a QR collection for pairing devices.

NEW — Security tab: Security audit (breached passwords via k-anonymity — only 5 hash characters leave the device; weak, reused, missing 2FA, old → score and action plan); Link check against phishing (lookalikes of your sites, punycode, mixed alphabets, your brand as a subdomain — fully offline); Legacy package — an encrypted file with instructions for a trusted person.

Everything is kept only in an AES-256-GCM vault on the device (master password + biometrics) and is removed when you uninstall. Interface in 15 languages.
