Pupikes Doctor lets you photograph a visible problem or describe symptoms and, by comparing against a pre-downloaded reference library on-device (not an AI diagnosis), shows possible matches and general first-step advice. It is informational only and never diagnoses, prescribes treatment or replaces a doctor; in an emergency, seek immediate medical help.

The photo comparison runs on the device against a reference library the app downloads once. The camera image is processed locally and is not uploaded by us. Your chosen language and inputs stay local; there is no account.

Everything runs on your device with no account and no ads; the interface supports 15 languages.

Version 1.0023: the photo is now examined by a built-in neural model (MobileNetV3-Small, about 3 MB, runs entirely on the device, no internet) that compares it with a library of 18,498 reference images, including 100+ checked photos for each of the 25 conditions, and shows the three most similar conditions with percentages ("looks like: sunburn 62 %, rash 21 %…"). On a phone with little memory, or if the model cannot load, the app says so and uses the older fingerprint comparison. Every result card has a "More about this" section with links in your language (Wikipedia in your language and in English, MedlinePlus, NHS, MSD Manuals) that open in the browser — the app carries no description databases, while the built-in short data for the 25 common conditions stays. Optional setting (off by default): "Connect to the Pupikes server" — when you confirm "this was X", only the numeric fingerprint of the photo is sent, never the photo itself; the app also receives new fingerprints from the server.
