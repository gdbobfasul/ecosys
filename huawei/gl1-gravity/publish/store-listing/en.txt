Gravity Leap is a physics slingshot game: hop from planet to planet by riding each world's gravity. Time your thrust to swing off along the tangent and reach the next planet across 16 hand-tuned levels with orbiting moons, asteroid belts, black holes and a gravity slingshot. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
