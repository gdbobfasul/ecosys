## For reviewer

To address the review, a new offline "Tools" (Инструменти) section was added with real developer/text utilities: a live regex tester (matches, groups, count), a JSON/CSV formatter & converter (pretty-print, validate, JSON↔CSV), and text encoders (URL, HTML-entity, base64 encode/decode). All tools are fully offline, work without an account or network, and are localized. This adds distinctive, deeper functionality that differentiates the app.
