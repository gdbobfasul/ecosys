Privacy Policy

App package: com.pupikes.aip1guardian.hw · Store: HUAWEI AppGallery · Provider: Dai Grup Ltd.

Silent Guardian is a discreet personal-safety companion for moments when you may not be able to speak up. Jot down a calm note about your day — a secret word or phrase you choose, once it appears in that note, acts as a silent alarm that nobody around you notices. Pick a situation template (a general situation, an elderly person who has fallen or is unresponsive, domestic abuse, or a lone worker), add what you heard or what was said, and get a clear AI-assisted risk read — Observation, Attention, Alert or Critical — together with a ready, editable message you can send to your trusted contacts. A single silent button sends a quiet call for help with no sound and no visible change for the people around you, and a check-in timer raises an alert if you do not confirm you are safe in time. It works with built-in logic on your device; optionally you can add your own AI key (for example an Anthropic key) or a proxy endpoint, kept only locally, for a deeper AI-assisted read. IMPORTANT: this is a prototype safety companion — sending alerts is SIMULATED and this is NOT a real emergency service; if you are in real danger, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU). No account, no tracking, in 15 languages.

1. We do not collect personal data

This application has no user accounts, no registration and no login by us. We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings and app data (such as the interface language you choose, and any content the app keeps for you) are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Network

The app uses the internet for its stated functions. The app works on your device with built-in logic and has no accounts, ads or third-party tracking. Your note, trusted contacts and any optional home location are stored only locally on your device. Optionally you may add your own AI provider key (for example an Anthropic key) or a proxy endpoint; it is stored only locally on your device and is never embedded in the app. Only when you request an analysis, and only if you have configured such a key/endpoint, the context you chose to analyze is sent to the AI provider you selected to return the risk read; nothing else is transmitted, and without a key the app stays fully offline. IMPORTANT: this is a prototype — the sending of alerts to contacts is SIMULATED in the app and this is NOT a real emergency or dispatch service; in real danger, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU).

4. Children

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
