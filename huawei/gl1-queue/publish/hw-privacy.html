Climb the stairs Joe is a one-tap timing game set on an endless staircase rising through the clouds. Tap the bottom of the screen to climb one step forward; tap the top to overtake, a riskier move whose reward keeps growing. Catch up to the figure ahead and you push on together. Keep your rhythm to clear all 15 hand-tuned levels of drifting fog, marble steps and crumbling cracks and reach the loop at the top. One-handed touch controls, fully offline, no accounts.
