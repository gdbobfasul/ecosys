Pupikes Toolkit Videos makes HOW-TO VIDEOS on your phone. Shoot or pick a few clips and photos as steps — the app arranges them as chapters (Step 1, 2, 3…), adds a caption to every step in any of 15 languages, arrows, circles and underlines on the frame, a title screen and a final recap screen, an optional voice-over (microphone recording or built-in speech synthesis) and generated background music. From one project you export a video lesson (MP4), a GIF guide and a printable instruction sheet (PDF/PNG) with the frames and the text. Templates: repair, recipe, product/review, exercise.

New video operations, all on the device: trim (start/duration), audio only → MP3, rotate and mirror, speed 0.5×–3×, remove sound, downscale to 720p/480p, compress to a smaller file, frame → JPG photo, GIF with your own seconds/fps/width — plus the MP4/WebM/AVI/MOV/MKV/GIF converter.

The tools run fully on-device. Your files and settings stay local. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Everything runs on your device with no account and no ads; the interface supports 15 languages.
