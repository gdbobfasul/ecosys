## For reviewer

To address the "single feature" note, a new "Tools" (Инструменти) tab has been added with three real, fully-offline utilities: a CBT thought-diary (5-step cognitive reframing with local save/export), a distress scale (0–100 SUDS tracker with history and average), and a mood-activities list (behavioral activation with progress). These are in addition to the diary and analytics. The app is no longer single-purpose. All content is localized into 15 languages and works offline without an account.
